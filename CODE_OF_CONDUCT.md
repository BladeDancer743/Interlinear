# Community code of conduct

Interlinear welcomes precise disagreement. Technical corrections are part of the project; hostility is not.

## Expected behavior

- Critique the text, evidence, or behavior—not the person.
- Cite sources when correcting terminology or physics.
- State uncertainty and distinguish convention from fact.
- Respect different language backgrounds and levels of domain knowledge.
- Avoid sharing private papers, credentials, or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Deliberate misinformation or fabricated citations.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Repeated disruption after a maintainer asks for a discussion to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile or repository security reporting channel. Good-faith reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
